Business recommendations

  1. Cap discounts on Tables and Bookcases — both run at a net loss overall, driven by discounts averaging above 20%.
  2. Set a hard discount ceiling around 20% in the quoting workflow — average margin turns negative past that point and keeps falling.
  3. Audit the Central region specifically — its profit margin trails the West by roughly half, despite comparable or higher sales volume.
  4. Review loss-making accounts before renewal — around one in five customers is net loss-making across their order history.
  5. Plan Q4 inventory and staffing ahead of time — the November–December spike repeats every year in this data.
